# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who made the call
- **Supersedes:** ADR-XXXX, or none
- **Superseded by:** ADR-YYYY, or none
- **References:** PRs, review findings, or design notes, if any

## Context

What problem forced a decision, and what constraints apply. A few sentences.

## Decision

What we will do, stated in the present tense.

## Rejected options

- **Option.** Why it lost.

## Consequences

What changes because of this decision: follow-up work, costs, and risks we accept.
